A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) entry for the **Two Lines to Your Past Self** bounty: a program that writes exactly two lines — a warning to your past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (269 characters, two lines, deterministic — both runs write the same bytes).

## The entry

> Don't delete the voicemail your grandmother leaves on your twenty-first birthday, telling you she's proud of you — save it.
> That thirty-second message is the only recording of her voice you will ever own, and on the nights you want to quit, it is the reason you don't.
